# Code Of Conduct

AgentKB contributors are expected to keep discussions and contributions respectful, specific, and focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer rather than escalating them in a public thread.
